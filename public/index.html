    Before you build +

    This is an indoor scenic prototype, not a structural or wind-rated wall. Do not lean on it or hang equipment from it.

    Validate joinery, support connections, ballast and floor grip in the final layout. The taller panel has its own frame and jacks; it is not the 8′ panel stretched.

    The angle control explores layouts. Set and secure the wings before dressing the corners. Change the angle only after removing corner finishes and re-securing supports.

    Mobile textures approximate materials and paint. Check real samples under your lighting.
