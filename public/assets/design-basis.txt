LAYOUT
Four 4-foot panels form the 16-foot back wall. Two 4-foot panels per wing
match Jeff's Blender additions. Two wings turn inward together from 0 to
90 degrees; default 45 degrees.
Default design: 10x4 panels at 45 degrees, square 10-inch platform 12
from the walls, painted plywood floor under it, all surfaces charcoal. Front corner lines remain coincident.
Floor is assumed 8 feet deep and follows the footprint. This represents
recutting/reconfiguring the floor and re-dressing corner finishes, not
moving a finished floor/wall assembly while occupied or ballasted.

SEAMLESS CYC RETROFIT — OCTOBER 13, 2026
Two independent design options: rounded vertical corners and wall-to-floor
perimeter coves. Both default off. Retrofit cleats fasten over the existing
wall facing into sound framing; add rear blocking where required. Existing
wall connectors, completed panel/jack counts and prior work remain intact.
Actual 2x4 backing is 1.5 x 3.5 inches. Never use thin wall skin alone as an anchor.
Vertical face radius: 36 inches. Floor face radius: 24 inches. These are project
choices, not universal standards. Rib templates fit the actual inside angle
(180 minus wing angle); zero-degree wings have no vertical corner inserts.
Corners alone continue straight to the floor. With both options, separately
fitted tapered gores over radial ribs form two compound lower junctions.
Use a full-size mockup and approved bendable skin, not an assumption that any
laminate accepts this bend, adhesive or finish. Support every joint; fair with
thin filler coats rather than filling large unsupported gaps with plaster.
Floor coves need a stable paintable transition substrate. Do not plaster onto
or pin floating click-lock flooring; keep its expansion gap clear. Coving and
feathered compound are decorative surfaces that must not carry feet or staging.

The shared plan reserves 24 inches sweep + 6 inches feather + 2 inches clear
space: 32 inches minimum platform gap for perimeter only. Compound corners need
33 inches at 45-degree wings, 34 inches at 90 degrees. Vertical corners alone
need insert clearance (5 inches at 45 degrees, 12 inches at 90). Angle-dependent
minimum = ceil(36 - (36 - floor radius - margin) x cos(wing angle / 2)) when
vertical corners are present; margin is 8 inches with perimeter, otherwise 2.
These are effective platform gaps. Requested gap settings stay stored and return
when the corresponding option is removed. Pricing, cuts, model and guide use the
same effective plan. Skin joint lines, shapes and thicknesses remain illustrative;
field template every junction before batch cutting.

Corner ribs sit directly on the wall crossbars. Added crossbars, cut from the same
stock as the toggles (1x3 on 10-foot flats, 1x2 on 8-foot), go midway between the
existing ones (12, 36, 60, 84 and, on 10-foot flats, 108 inches above the subfloor)
in the two panels at each rounded corner, plus one low crossbar behind the bottom
rib when the bottom rail is not already there. They are glued to the back of the
skin, toe-screwed into the stiles and stapled through the face. Each rib is
centered on its crossbar and screwed from behind the wall, two screws per wall,
through the crossbar and skin into the rib edge: #9 x 3-1/2 inch on 10-foot flats
(1x3 on edge, about 7/8 inch in the rib), #9 x 3 inch on 8-foot (1x2, about
1-3/8 inch). Nothing is fastened to the wall face: no cleats, no vertical rear
corner backers (they could not pass the toggles) and no backer behind the curve's
vertical skin joint, which lands on the ribs instead. 11 ribs per corner on
10-foot walls with a painted floor.
Ribs: 3/4-inch plywood, up to 16-inch centers for simple curves; radial junction
ribs spaced approximately 8 inches around the upper arc. Purchasing envelopes
allow two 36-inch vertical rib blanks or three 26-inch floor/junction blanks per
4x8 sheet; verify real nesting after the sample. Flexible skin sheets and compound
gore stock include separate waste allowances. Specific flexible skin, rib plywood,
adhesive and compound reinforcement products/prices remain pending.
