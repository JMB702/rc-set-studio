Liquid Rubber's polyurethane deck system explicitly includes plywood and indoor utility/basement floors. Its reinforced seam treatment uses its primer and 4-inch geotextile. However, the manufacturer says the fabric stays visible. The caulk-only alternative trades away reinforcement and carries greater cracking risk. This does not meet an invisible-seam requirement. Westcoat ALX Interior is an actual interior floor topping system for plywood. Its specification calls for at least 3/4-inch exterior-grade or CDX tongue-and-groove plywood, blocked and fastened, and a suitable dry substrate. The proposed overlay must be checked against those requirements; permanent attachment alone is insufficient. The reinforced build uses WP-40 seam membrane, WP-25 or WP-30 lath and WP-10 staples, modified cement layers, then the specified finish and sealers. This is a professional-use system. No Home Depot listing or local price was verified. An installer/distributor quote is needed; do not invent quantities or substitute unrelated primer/paint. A continuous finish is not a guarantee against movement or installation defects.
