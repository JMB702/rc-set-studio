RC SET STUDIO — DESIGN BASIS / OCTOBER 5, 2026

LAYOUT
Four 4-foot panels form the 16-foot back wall. Two 4-foot panels per wing
match Jeff's Blender additions. Two wings turn inward together from 0 to
90 degrees; default 45 degrees. Front corner lines remain coincident.
Floor is assumed 8 feet deep and follows the footprint. This represents
recutting/reconfiguring the floor and re-dressing corner finishes, not
moving a finished floor/wall assembly while occupied or ballasted.

All lumber dimensions below are ACTUAL, in inches. Buy solid straight
lumber whose measured dimensions match; product names alone are not enough.

8 x 4 PANEL (REVISED FLUSH HARDWARE)
2 stiles .75 x 3.5 x 96; 2 rails .75 x 1.5 x 46.5;
3 toggles .75 x 1.5 x 46.5 at centers 24/48/72.
Skin .106 x 48 x 96. Outside diagonals sqrt(48^2+96^2)=107.331.

10 x 4 PANEL — NEW PROTOTYPE, NOT AN 8-FOOT PANEL SCALED UP
2 full-length stiles .75 x 5.5 x 120 (trim solid 12-foot stock).
2 rails + 3 toggles .75 x 2.5 x 46.5; centers 24/48/72.
1 seam backer .75 deep x 3.5 high x 46.5 long, centered at height96.
Backer height94.25 to97.75; aligned with skin rear face, supporting both
sheets. Two .106-thick skins:48x96 plus48x24. No structural stile splice.
Frame width .75+46.5+.75=48. Diagonal sqrt(48^2+120^2)=129.244.
Fourteen #6 x1.5 frame screws (2 at each seam-backer end), plus glue.
Skin glue and half-inch narrow crown staples at roughly4-inch centers,
centered on wood. Staple into both edges of backed skin joint. Test on scrap.

Three25lb bags shown per panel are illustrative preliminary ballast only.
A prior modest10lbf disturbance calculation is not structural approval.
Validate connections, sideways stability, tipping, sliding and ballast
on the real floor, at each layout, before use. No wind, leaning, doors or
hung equipment are within this prototype scope.

STRAIGHT WALL CONNECTIONS
Flush skins and mating stiles. Four #8x1.25 flat-head wood screws per seam
at12/36/60/84;10ft panels add108. Screw axis0.75 behind skin rear face;
predrill clearance first stile/pilot second; clamp faces aligned first.
This set has5 straight seams:3 across back and1 within each wing.
Corners are variable-angle layout joints; final corner connector choice
and attachment require scenic-shop review. This is not a verified hinge.

FINISHES
Render uses one unbroken charcoal surface per back/wing, including over
the10ft skin joint. Practical scenic seam fabric and primer/paint need a
fixed assembly; do not rigid-plaster a movable corner. Refinish after moves.
Oak floor:glueless click-lock;3/8-inch expansion beneath wall-fixed trim.
Charcoal:fully supported smooth plywood overlay, compatible defects/joint
repair, wood primer, then floor paint. Seamless coating across moving
plywood is not guaranteed; the seam system remains a test/compatibility
item. Do not use underlayment patch as an assumed exposed wear finish.
The visualization is dimensionally derived, not a structural certification.
