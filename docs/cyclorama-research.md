# Cyclorama retrofit research — October 13, 2026

The existing walls remain assembled. The design adds independent seamless wall-corner and wall-to-floor options; the existing wall facing is not assumed to be rated for bending. Geometry and purchasing allowances are generated by `public/cyclorama.js`.

## Construction evidence

[Spokane Productions, Building the Infinity Wall](https://spokaneproductions.com/building-the-infinity-wall/) documents 24-inch floor sweeps cut from ¾-inch plywood, runners and intermediate support, then flexible hardboard. CNC nesting helped repeatable ribs. Their connected pointed panel cracked under compound bending; separate tapered pieces worked. Multiple filling and sanding passes were needed, with a flexible rubber tool in the lower corner. They also reported panel warping from a moisture-heavy floor adhesive. This supports separate, templated gores on radial support and a dry-fit/adhesion sample. It does not establish that an arbitrary laminate or plywood will bend successfully, nor provide corner person-hours.

[Ambanja Films, Studio Build](https://ambanjafilms.co.uk/studio-build/) identifies the intersections of curves as the difficult part and describes bringing in help. Their plywood/MDF construction used plaster, sanding, primer and matte paint. No task-level timings are reported.

[Pro Cyc, Tips for Building a Professional Cyclorama Wall](https://www.procyc.com/tips-for-building-a-professional-cyclorama-wall/) gives only an overall DIY duration of days and promotes modular systems. It offers an alternative to obtain a quote, not evidence for a particular labor allowance on this retrofit.

## Design and time decisions

The 36-inch vertical radius and 24-inch floor radius are project design choices, not a universal standard. The larger vertical radius avoids a zero-radius toe at the two compound corners. Templates must follow the actual wing angle: a 90-degree rib only fits a 90-degree corner. Corners without perimeter continue to the floor without compound junctions.

Add cleats through existing facing into sound framing, with rear blocking where attachment points miss framing. The thin existing face alone is not the attachment substrate. Maintain the structural wall connectors; the overlay replaces exposed corner caulk, not the connectors. Use a full-size bending sample before ordering skin; confirm material, bend direction, radius, fastener pull-through and compatible coating/adhesive. Paper tape must lie flat; compound curves may require other compatible reinforcement, kept pending until the mockup.

The two lower junctions receive **24 person-hours each, 48 total** for templates, radial ribs, separate fitted gores, repeated thin filling, sanding and raking-light corrections. This deliberately conservative planning allowance is our estimate, **not a published benchmark**. Straight sweeps, upper corner skin and final coating are additional tasks. Only two effective workers are assumed in the junction phase, so a large crew cannot divide this work to implausibly short elapsed time; the estimate conservatively prices the whole configured crew on site. Allow **3–5 additional drying calendar days**, product and climate dependent, with possible overlap with other work. No drying hours are billed as labor. Time the first corner and revise the forecast.

The stage has at least a **32-inch effective back and side gap** for perimeter only: 24-inch sweep projection + 6-inch feather allowance + 2-inch clear space. With both options, the compound-corner toe requires extra clearance: 33 inches at 45-degree wings and 34 inches at 90 degrees. Corners only also enforce insert clearance (5 inches at 45 degrees, 12 inches at 90 degrees). The shared plan calculates the angle-dependent minimum, including the full curve and feather rather than only the straight sweep. This keeps the stage off the decorative curve and finish; the cove is not a load-bearing walking surface. Requested gaps remain saved and return as options are disabled.

## Simpler alternatives and pricing

1. Corners only avoid the entire lower compound transition.
2. Make one verified master rib and use a router pattern, or get a CNC nesting/cutting quote. Do not reduce the labor estimate until a real quote and workflow are selected.
3. Request a prefab compound-corner quote matching the actual wing angle, 36-inch vertical radius and 24-inch floor radius. A standard 90-degree kit is not automatically compatible with angled wings or these radii.
