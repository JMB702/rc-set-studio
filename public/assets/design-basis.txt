RC SET STUDIO — DESIGN BASIS / OCTOBER 7, 2026 / SCREW-ONLY JACK REVISION

LAYOUT
Four 4-foot panels form the 16-foot back wall. Two 4-foot panels per wing
match Jeff's Blender additions. Two wings turn inward together from 0 to
90 degrees; default 45 degrees.
Default design: 10x4 panels at 45 degrees, square 10-inch platform 12
from the walls, painted plywood floor under it, all surfaces charcoal. Front corner lines remain coincident.
Floor is assumed 8 feet deep and follows the footprint. This represents
recutting/reconfiguring the floor and re-dressing corner finishes, not
moving a finished floor/wall assembly while occupied or ballasted.

The guides lead with store lumber sizes and exact cut lengths. Technical
geometry below retains actual dimensions for design reference.
All lumber dimensions below are ACTUAL, in inches. Buy solid straight
lumber whose measured dimensions match; product names alone are not enough.

FLAT/JACK SIDE LAP — SCREW-ONLY PROTOTYPE
8-foot jack front 1.75 behind skin rear face; screw axis 2.625 behind it;
heights 12/22/32/42/52/62. 10-foot jack front 3.75 behind skin rear face;
screw axis 4.625 behind it; heights 12/26/40/54/68/82.
Overlap depth 1.75. Six #8 x 1.25 flat-head WOOD screws per jack (12 per
panel), driven from the outside stile into the upright. Both members are
actual .75 thick, leaving .5 nominal penetration in the receiving upright
and .25 to its far face. Do not over-countersink. Clamp first; clearance
hole in the stile only, manufacturer-appropriate pilot in the upright.
Test fit on scrap. Use sound wood and replace wood with stripped holes.
Do not substitute nails or staples for these jack screws. No nut/washer
assemblies, through-bolts or wrenches are needed. The attachment remains a
prototype; screw count is not a verified capacity or equivalent rating.
Retain support while fitting/removing the jacks, then verify the assembled
panel and ballast in the intended layout before releasing it.
Total #8 x 1.25 per panel: 12 jack + 8 diagonal = 20. Add 4 per straight
8-foot seam or 5 per 10-foot seam. Five straight seams in the eight-panel
set: 180 / 185 screws, respectively, bought as two 100-packs.

FINISHES
Render uses one unbroken charcoal surface per back/wing, including over
the10ft skin joint. Practical scenic seam fabric and primer/paint need a
fixed assembly; do not rigid-plaster a movable corner. Refinish after moves.
Oak floor:glueless click-lock;3/8-inch expansion beneath wall-fixed trim.
Charcoal show floor: two 23/32-inch plywood layers, approximately 1 7/16 inches
combined, over continuously supporting concrete. Upper joints offset in both
axes. Full-spread plywood-compatible adhesive and screws join the layers.
Thin Rapid Set SKIM FLOOR cement skim (planning average 1/16 inch), compatible
cementitious primer, then floor paint. No epoxy or polyurethane wear finish.
User chose limited performance traffic with patching and repainting accepted.
Paint alone over this cement underlayment is not a manufacturer-verified wear
system. Primer compatibility, adhesive/screw schedule and local product prices
remain pending. The retail skim bag listing and manufacturer TDS differ in size;
use verified pack yield when buying. Model finished-floor datum stays at zero.
The visualization is dimensionally derived, not a structural certification.

10-INCH PLATFORM (ADDED OCTOBER 6, 2026)
Optional and independent of the floor: none, angled or square. When a
floor is also chosen it runs under the platform, wall to wall, and the
platform stands on it. Finished top 10 above the floor it stands on.
Outline: the 8ft floor footprint, kept a set gap from the back wall line and
from both wing lines (each 0 to 48, default 12), out to the 96 front line.
Wing gaps are measured from the full wing line. The platform's sides have
their own angle, never wider than the walls: walls coming in push it in,
while it can turn further in on its own (walls 45, platform 90 = square).
Its sides keep the gap from both the real wings and their own angle line. An edge set flush (0 gap) against a wall gets no fascia.
Modules: each row of equal depth, no deeper than 48; each row divided into
equal columns no wider than 48. Small end pieces merge into a neighbor.
All lumber actual 1.5x3.5 (2x4). Rims stand on edge around each module,
butted pinwheel style; joists front-to-back at 16 centers. Legs: one in
every corner plus intermediates so no rim spans more than 48. Leg 9.422
(9 7/16) to the deck underside; 7.922 (7 15/16) where it stands on a sill.
Sills: 2x4 laid flat under every fascia edge. Deck: Plytanium 19/32 BC
sanded plywood, actual 0.578 x 47.938 x 95.938. Pair adjacent frame cells
within each row into larger deck pieces up to one 4x8 sheet, long direction
across the front-to-back joists. Default square: 4 decks over 8 frames,
4 sheets. Default 45-degree angled: 7 decks over 13 frames, 7 sheets.
The cut plan centers undersized stock over supporting rims, at most 3/64
allowance per edge; scribe angled boundaries to the joined frames.
Decks screwed (#8x2.5 at 6 around deck perimeters, 12 on interior rims and
joists), not glued. Remove spanning decks and fascia BEFORE separating the frames.
Frame seams retain #9x3 screws through adjoining rims, about every 12,
both sides. Tape follows deck-piece seams, not hidden frame joints.
Fascia: 0.106 lauan, 10 tall, glued and stapled to rims, legs and sills.
Finish: vinyl corner bead on top edges and outside corners, mesh tape on
deck seams and fascia joints, setting-type compound skim, sand, KILZ 2
cement-compatible primer and two coats floor paint; double plywood layers and full cement skim.
19/32 chosen over 3/4 for weight; framing at 16 centers carries the deck.
The top is walked on, but this is a scenic prototype, not a rated stage
deck: no load rating is claimed. Test the finish on a mockup; seams can
crack if modules move. Quantities in public/platform.js and pricing-calc.js.

VIEWER CONVENTIONS
Cut-list selection highlights matching visible parts in their current positions.
It does not move parts, replace the assembly or reframe the camera. Click the
row again, press Escape or close the cut list to clear the highlight. Frame-side-up and skin-side-up
bench steps keep the panel top at the same far end; turn it over sideways.

Platform fascia uses long strips cut along 8-foot lauan sheets, spanning frames.
Measure the actual floor-to-deck height (about 10 inches) and trim to fit.
Butt joints land on existing legs; corner ends include a trim allowance.
Default square layout: 6 side pieces instead of 12. Default angled: 12 instead of 19.
Cut lengths, stock packing, staples and joint tape follow these pieces; no bottom-edge tape.
